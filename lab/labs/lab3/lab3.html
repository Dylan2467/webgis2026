Layers

Use the layer switcher icon (top-right of the map) to toggle base maps and overlays on/off. Overlays included: elevation (WMS), land cover (WMS), US states (GeoJSON), today's earthquakes (GeoJSON), and active NWS weather alerts for NC (live geospatial web service feed).

Spatial Analysis: Buffer

Click the button, then click anywhere on the map to drop a point. A buffer polygon of the given radius will be generated around it using Turf.js.
